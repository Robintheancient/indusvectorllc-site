The future of blockchain in healthcare

Blockchain technology has the potential to transform many industries, including healthcare. Its ability to keep a secure, shared and tamper-evident record makes it well suited to some of the sector's hardest problems.

  1. Improving data security and interoperability. A shared, encrypted ledger can let providers exchange patient records securely while keeping a clear audit trail of who accessed what.
  2. Enhancing supply chain management. Tracking drugs and devices on a blockchain makes it harder for counterfeit or recalled products to reach patients.
  3. Enabling more efficient and effective clinical trials. Immutable records of protocols, consent and results improve transparency and trust in trial data.
  4. Enhancing patient engagement and empowerment. Patients can hold and share their own health data, deciding who sees it and when.

Blockchain is still maturing, but it is likely to become an important driver of innovation in healthcare.
